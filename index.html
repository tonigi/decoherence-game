View

Hue = phase, brightness = |ψ|. Orange tint = potential barrier, blue = well. Ghost preview shows the PT(t) curve for the current launch with no real-time actions. Polarization view: hue = polarization axis (red horizontal, green 45°, cyan vertical, violet −45°), white = circular. Optics show their axis on a dial: violet polarizer, amber rotator, teal plate, pink Stern–Gerlach magnet (axis goes up), red bomb station.

Sound: each horizontal band of the field is a note of a pentatonic scale, loud as √P in that band and panned by where its probability sits; faster packets sound brighter. The bells are Born-rule samples of |ψ|², a high halo grows with PT, a low beating drone with PM. Observation fades it all to silence.
